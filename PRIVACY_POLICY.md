# Privacy Policy — Wisp

**Last updated: September 14, 2026**

## Introduction

Wisp ("we", "our", "the app") is a meeting AI assistant developed by Boris Spiro. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use Wisp.

## Data Collection

### Audio Recordings

- Audio is recorded and stored **locally on your device** only.
- We do **not** upload, access, or store your audio recordings on any server.
- Audio files remain in the app's sandboxed storage and are deleted when you remove a meeting.

### Speech Recognition

Wisp transcribes in two stages, and they differ in where the audio is processed:

- **Live captions during a recording** use Apple's Speech Recognition service. Apple may process this audio on **their servers**, per [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Wisp never receives or stores it.
- **The final transcript** is produced **on your device**: by Wisp AI (an on-device Whisper model you download once) when enabled, or by Apple's on-device speech recogniser. No audio leaves the device for this stage.

We do not send audio anywhere ourselves, and we have no server that could receive it.

### On-Device AI (Apple Intelligence)

- On supported devices running iOS 26 or later, meeting notes and chat answers can be generated by **Apple Intelligence directly on your device**.
- In that mode nothing is sent to any AI provider, and no API key is needed.

### AI-Generated Notes

- Transcription text is sent to the AI provider **you choose** (OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, or DeepSeek) to generate meeting notes and answer chat questions.
- This happens **only when you have entered your own API key** and initiated a recording or import.
- **We ask first.** Before any meeting text is sent to a provider for the first time, Wisp shows a screen naming that provider, listing exactly what will be sent (the transcript text, your chat questions, the meeting notes as context, and your API key) and what will never be sent (your audio, your name, e-mail, Apple Account and contacts, and meetings you did not ask about). Nothing is transmitted unless you agree on that screen. Permission is per provider: switching providers asks again, and removing a key withdraws it. You can withdraw permission at any time by removing the key in Settings → "AI provider".
- We do not act as an intermediary — your device communicates directly with the AI provider's API.
- We do not store, access, or process your transcriptions on our servers.

### API Keys

- API keys you enter are stored in **Apple Keychain** on your device.
- Keys are never transmitted to us or any third party other than the AI provider you selected.
- Keys are protected with `kSecAttrAccessibleWhenUnlockedThisDeviceOnly`.

### Subscription Data

- Subscription purchases are processed by **Apple** through the App Store.
- We use StoreKit 2 to verify your subscription status locally.
- We do not collect payment information.

### Analytics

- Wisp uses **AppMetrica** (Yandex) to understand how the app is used and where it fails. Data is processed on AppMetrica servers.
- What is sent: which screens and features are used, how long a transcription took and on which engine, which errors occurred (as error codes, never as text), the chosen speech language and note template, subscription state, and completed purchases (product, price, currency).
- What is **never** sent: your audio, transcriptions, notes, chat messages, meeting titles, file names, and API keys. Analytics parameters carry counts, durations and fixed labels only.
- An anonymous device identifier generated by AppMetrica is used to count devices. Your name, e-mail, phone number and Apple ID are not collected, and analytics is not linked to your identity.
- Location collection and the advertising identifier (IDFA) are **disabled** in the SDK configuration. Wisp does **not** use App Tracking Transparency because it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
- **You can turn analytics off**: Settings → Analytics → "Share anonymous usage data". Switching it off stops everything described above, including crash reporting.
- Independently of that switch, the AppMetrica SDK reports its own operation (SDK version, delivery errors) to Yandex under AppMetrica's own identifier. That stream carries no data about you or your meetings.
- AppMetrica privacy policy: https://appmetrica.io/about/privacy

### Crash Diagnostics

- If the app crashes or freezes, iOS may hand Wisp a diagnostic report through Apple's **MetricKit**. These reports are written to a file **on your device** and are never transmitted anywhere; they are shared only if **you** choose to send one from Settings.
- Separately, AppMetrica collects crash and performance reports (stack traces, device model, OS version, app version) unless you turn analytics off. A stack trace contains no meeting content.

## Data Storage

All of your content is stored locally on your device:

- Meeting recordings (audio files)
- Transcriptions
- AI-generated notes
- Chat messages
- App settings and preferences

**We operate no servers of our own, and we cannot read your recordings, transcriptions or notes.** Usage statistics described above are processed by AppMetrica on our behalf and contain none of that content.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your data and we do not share your content with anyone. External communication occurs when:

1. **You** send a transcription to an AI provider (OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, or DeepSeek) using **your own API key**.
2. **Apple** processes subscription purchases through the App Store, and may process live-caption audio for speech recognition.
3. **You** download the on-device Wisp AI model from Hugging Face, which serves the model files.
4. **Wisp** sends anonymous usage and crash statistics to AppMetrica, unless you have turned analytics off in Settings.

## Data Deletion

- You can delete any meeting (including its audio, transcription, notes, and chat history) at any time from within the app.
- Uninstalling the app removes all data permanently.
- Auto-delete settings allow automatic removal of old meetings after 30, 60, or 90 days.

## Children's Privacy

Wisp is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
